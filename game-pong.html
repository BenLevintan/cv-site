About This Game

Two paddles. One ball. Pure reflex. The game that started it all, rebuilt in the browser.
ONE BALL. INFINITE TENSION.
Control your paddle. Predict the bounce. Outsmart the AI opponent. The ball gets faster with every hit. Zero loading screens. Zero microtransactions. Zero loot boxes.
